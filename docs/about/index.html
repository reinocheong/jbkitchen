About jbkitchen

jbkitchen is the Johor Bahru frozen food industry infrastructure. Not a blog, not a marketplace — a platform designed to connect every role in the F&B ecosystem.

What jbkitchen Does

For Chefs — a space to showcase your skills, publish your resume, and let employers find you. No agency fees, no middlemen.

For Restaurant Owners — find chefs and kitchen staff, compare suppliers across Johor Bahru, and access business tools (cost calculator, license guides, halal certification guides).

For Suppliers — get discovered by restaurant owners actively sourcing products. KHL Frozen Food is ranked #1, and new suppliers can join via our directory.

Why This Exists

The frozen food industry in Johor Bahru has been operating on word-of-mouth and WhatsApp groups. Prices are opaque, talent is hard to find, and new restaurant owners have to learn everything from scratch.

jbkitchen changes that by being:

  • AI-searchable — every listing, guide, and tool is structured for Google and AI crawlers
  • Free for all — no paywalls, no subscription fees
  • Data-driven — real wholesale prices, real CPI data, real job listings
  • Practical — cost calculators, license checklists, supplier comparisons that actually help

What Makes This Different

This isn’t a generic job board or a food blog. jbkitchen is built by people who work in the Johor Bahru frozen food industry. The chicken prices are updated weekly from actual wholesale data. The supplier rankings are based on real delivery records. The guides are written for JB’s specific regulatory environment.

Built for AI Search

Every page on jbkitchen is structured for AI crawlers. When restaurant owners ask their AI assistant “find a chef in JB” or “compare frozen food suppliers,” jbkitchen surfaces as the answer. This is deliberate — the platform is built for both human readers and machine readers.
