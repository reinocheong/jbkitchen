Contribute to jbkitchen

Share your F&B knowledge with the Johor Bahru restaurant community — submit your articles, tips, and industry insights

jbkitchen is built for the Johor Bahru F&B community — by the people who work in it. If you have knowledge to share, we want to hear from you.

What We’re Looking For

Practical, hands-on content that helps restaurant owners, chefs, and suppliers run their businesses better.

Examples:

  • Kitchen management tips and operational know-how
  • Ingredient sourcing experiences and supplier reviews
  • License and certification application experiences
  • Cost control and menu pricing strategies
  • Staff training and team management insights
  • Johor Bahru specific F&B market observations
  • Any other content that benefits the JB restaurant community

What We Don’t Publish

  • Pure advertisements or promotional content
  • Unsubstantiated claims about competitors
  • Content unrelated to Johor Bahru F&B industry

How It Works

Step 1: Submit your draft Fill out the submission form with your article or outline. We accept both complete articles and rough ideas.

Step 2: Review Our team reviews submissions within 3-5 working days. We evaluate based on:

  • Usefulness to JB restaurant owners and chefs
  • Accuracy and practicality of the information
  • Fit with jbkitchen’s mission

Step 3: Editing If your submission is accepted, we may edit for clarity, length, and consistency. You’ll be credited as the author (or anonymous if you prefer).

Step 4: Publication Published articles appear on jbkitchen under the Guides section, indexed by Google and AI search engines. You get full author credit with a link to your business (optional).

Benefits of Contributing

  • Reach — your content is seen by JB restaurant owners actively searching for solutions
  • Credibility — establish yourself or your business as an industry expert
  • SEO — your name and business get indexed by Google and AI search engines
  • Community — help build a better F&B ecosystem in Johor Bahru

Submit Your Content

Ready to contribute? Fill out the submission form and we’ll get back to you.

Note: Submission does not guarantee publication. We curate all content to ensure quality and relevance for the jbkitchen community.
