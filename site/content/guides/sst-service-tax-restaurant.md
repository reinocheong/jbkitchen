## SST 2.0 Overview for F&B Businesses

**Sales and Service Tax (SST)** was reintroduced in Malaysia on 1 September 2018, replacing the Goods and Services Tax (GST). SST consists of two separate taxes:

- **Service Tax** — charged on taxable services, including restaurant and food services
- **Sales Tax** — charged on manufactured goods, including frozen food products

For the F&B industry, understanding which tax applies to your business — and when — is critical to staying compliant with the **Royal Malaysian Customs Department (RMCD)**.

## Service Tax for Restaurants

### Who Must Register?

You must register for service tax if your restaurant's **annual turnover from taxable food and beverage services exceeds RM500,000**.

The RM500,000 threshold applies to **total food and beverage sales**, not per outlet. If you operate multiple restaurant branches under the same registered business entity, combine the turnover of all outlets.

If your turnover is below RM500,000, registration is **voluntary** — but once registered, you must charge, collect, and remit service tax regardless of turnover.

### Service Tax Rate

| Category | Rate |
|---|---|
| Restaurant, café, bar, pub, catering | **6%** |
| Other taxable services (non-F&B) | **8%** |

The government maintained the **6% rate for F&B services** even when the general service tax rate increased to 8% in 2024 — this is a deliberate measure to keep dining affordable.

### What Is Taxable?

Service tax at 6% applies to:

- **Dine-in** meals and beverages — YES, taxable
- **Takeaway and self-pickup** orders — YES, taxable (the service is the preparation of food, not just the dining experience)
- **Delivery orders** placed through your own ordering system — YES, taxable
- **Catering services** (on-site and off-site) — YES, taxable
- **Private dining and event hosting** — YES, taxable
- **Banquet and function packages** — YES, taxable

### What Is NOT Taxable?

- **Manufacturing of food products** (this falls under sales tax, not service tax)
- **Wholesale supply of ingredients** between businesses (no service element)
- Raw ingredient sales (e.g., selling unbranded flour, sugar, oil to another business)

## SST on Food Delivery Platforms (GrabFood, Foodpanda, ShopeeFood)

This is a common point of confusion. Here is how it works:

When a customer orders through **GrabFood, Foodpanda, or ShopeeFood**:

- **The delivery platform** is the party providing the taxable service to the customer
- **The platform charges and collects** the 6% service tax from the customer
- **The restaurant** does NOT add separate service tax on these orders (the platform handles it)
- The restaurant receives the net food amount from the platform

In other words: **You do not charge SST on top of GrabFood orders.** The platform already includes it in the customer's bill.

*Exception:* If you run your own delivery service (in-house riders) and issue your own invoice/receipt to the customer, then you must charge 6% service tax.

## Sales Tax for Frozen Food Manufacturers

If you manufacture frozen food products in Malaysia, sales tax may apply.

### Threshold and Rate

| Criteria | Detail |
|---|---|
| Annual turnover threshold | **RM1,500,000** |
| Sales tax rate (general) | **10%** |
| Sales tax rate (certain food products) | **5% or exempt** |

Most basic food items are **exempt from sales tax** under the Sales Tax (Goods Exempted From Sales Tax) Order. This includes:

- Fresh and frozen meat, fish, and seafood
- Fresh vegetables and fruits
- Rice, flour, sugar, cooking oil
- Essential food ingredients

However, **processed and prepared frozen foods** (e.g., frozen ready meals, frozen snacks, frozen desserts) may be subject to sales tax at **5% or 10%** depending on their tariff classification. Check your product's HS (Harmonised System) code with Customs or your tax agent.

## Registration Process

### Service Tax Registration (Restaurants)

1. Go to the **MySST Portal**: [https://mysst.customs.gov.my](https://mysst.customs.gov.my)
2. Register for a MySST account (requires business registration number, director's IC, and contact details)
3. Complete the online registration form, selecting "Restaurant / Food & Beverage" as the taxable service category
4. Submit and wait for approval — typically **5–10 working days**
5. Upon approval, you receive your SST registration number
6. Begin issuing tax invoices showing the SST breakdown

### Sales Tax Registration (Manufacturers)

1. Register via the same **MySST Portal**
2. Select "Manufacturer" as the registration type
3. Declare your manufacturing activity and product categories with HS codes
4. Approval timeline: **2–4 weeks** (manufacturing registrations require more review)
5. Upon approval, you receive a sales tax license number

### Registration Deadline

You must register within **30 days** of exceeding the turnover threshold. Late registration attracts penalties.

## Filing and Payment

### Filing Frequency

Both service tax and sales tax returns must be filed **every 2 months** (bimonthly).

| Taxable Period | Filing Due Date |
|---|---|
| January–February | 31 March |
| March–April | 31 May |
| May–June | 31 July |
| July–August | 30 September |
| September–October | 30 November |
| November–December | 31 January (following year) |

### How to File

Filing is done entirely online through the **MySST Portal**. You declare:

- Total value of taxable sales for the period
- Total SST collected
- Any adjustments or exemptions
- Payment amount due

Payment can be made through:

- **FPX online banking** (direct from the MySST portal)
- **Bank transfer** to Customs' designated account
- **E-banking** through your bank's bill payment feature

### Penalty for Late Filing

| Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Late filing (SST-02 return) | Up to RM50,000 or imprisonment up to 3 years, or both |
| Late payment | 10% penalty on unpaid amount for the first 30 days; additional 15% (total 25%) if unpaid after 60 days |
| Underdeclaration/evasion | 200% of the tax underpaid |

These penalties are significant. Set reminders for your filing dates — missing a deadline is expensive.

## SST Invoicing Requirements

Your tax invoices/receipts must clearly show:

1. Your business name, address, and SST registration number
2. Invoice date and serial number
3. Customer details (name and address, for B2B invoices)
4. Description of goods or services
5. Itemised price before tax
6. **Service tax amount at 6%** (shown as a separate line)
7. Total amount payable including tax

For restaurants, this typically appears as:

```
Subtotal:              RM100.00
Service Tax 6%:        RM  6.00
Total:                 RM106.00
```

*Note: Service charge (usually 10%) is a business charge, not a government tax. SST is calculated on the food price before service charge.*

## SST on Catering Services

For catering businesses:

- **On-site catering** (you cook and serve at the client's venue) — service tax at **6%** applies if your annual turnover exceeds RM500,000
- **Off-site catering** (food prepared at your kitchen, delivered and set up) — also subject to **6%** service tax under the same threshold
- **Event packages** including food + venue + service — the food component is taxable; non-food services (venue rental) may be subject to **8%** service tax if provided as a separate service

If you provide both catering AND food manufacturing (e.g., frozen food line), you may need to register for both service tax and sales tax.

## FAQ

### Is takeaway subject to service tax?

**Yes.** Service tax applies to the preparation and serving of food and beverages, whether consumed on the premises or taken away. The tax is on the **service of preparing food**, not the physical location of consumption. All takeaway orders from registered restaurants attract 6% service tax.

### Do I charge service tax on GrabFood, Foodpanda, or ShopeeFood orders?

**No — the platform does it.** When customers order through third-party delivery platforms, the platform is the responsible party for collecting and remitting service tax. You do not add service tax on top of the food price you charge the platform. The platform includes SST in the total billed to the customer.

### What if my restaurant turnover is below RM500,000?

If your annual turnover from food and beverage services is **below RM500,000**, you are **not required** to register for service tax. You do not charge service tax to your customers and do not need to file SST returns. However:

- Monitor your turnover monthly — if you exceed the threshold mid-year, you must register within 30 days
- Voluntary registration is allowed if you want to claim the status (some corporate clients prefer to deal with SST-registered vendors)

### Do I need to register for both service tax and sales tax?

**Possibly.** If you both (a) operate a restaurant exceeding RM500,000 turnover and (b) manufacture frozen food products exceeding RM1.5 million turnover, you need both registrations. They are separate registrations in the MySST portal with separate filing obligations.

### How do I know if my frozen food product is subject to sales tax?

Check the **HS (Harmonised System) tariff code** for your product. The Sales Tax (Goods Exempted From Sales Tax) Order lists exempt items. If your product is not listed as exempt, it falls under the standard rate (10%) or reduced rate (5%) depending on classification. Consult a **Customs-registered tax agent** for a definitive determination — incorrect classification can lead to penalties.

### Can I backdate my SST registration?

**No.** SST registration is not backdated. You must begin charging service tax from the date your registration is approved. If you exceeded the threshold 6 months ago and only registered now, you may face penalties for the unregistered period. If you anticipate crossing the threshold soon, register proactively.

### How do I handle SST as a frozen food wholesaler supplying to restaurants?

If you only wholesale (buy and resell without manufacturing), you are generally dealing with **service tax**:

- If your wholesale turnover exceeds RM500,000, you must register for service tax at **6%** (or be registered under the appropriate service category)
- Many B2B wholesale food transactions are **exempt** if you are not providing a "service" — check with Customs

If you manufacture frozen food (producing, not just trading), **sales tax** rules apply with the RM1.5 million threshold.

### Can I claim back SST on my business purchases?

**Service tax** — generally **no**. Unlike GST, SST is a single-stage tax. Businesses cannot claim input tax credits on service tax paid. This is one reason restaurant operating costs increased after the shift from GST to SST.

**Sales tax** — registered manufacturers can apply for sales tax exemption on raw materials and packaging materials used in manufacturing, under specific Customs schemes. This requires separate application and approval through the MySST portal.
