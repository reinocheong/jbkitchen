## Overview: HACCP, ISO 22000, and GMP

Food safety certifications are not all created equal. For Malaysian food businesses, the three most commonly discussed standards are **GMP**, **HACCP**, and **ISO 22000**. Understanding what each one does — and which one your business actually needs — can save you tens of thousands of ringgit and months of unnecessary work.

## What Is GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)?

**GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice)** is the foundation of all food safety systems. It covers the basic environmental and operational conditions needed to produce safe food:

- Facility design and layout
- Equipment maintenance and calibration
- Personnel hygiene and training
- Cleaning and sanitation procedures
- Pest control
- Waste management
- Storage and transportation conditions

Think of GMP as the "housekeeping" standard. Most higher certifications (HACCP, ISO 22000) assume GMP is already in place. In Malaysia, GMP certification is offered by KKM as an independent scheme, separate from MeSTI, with costs ranging from **RM3,000 to RM8,000**.

## What Is HACCP?

**HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points)** is a systematic, science-based approach to identifying, evaluating, and controlling food safety hazards. It was developed by NASA and Pillsbury in the 1960s to ensure food safety for astronauts.

HACCP is built on **7 principles**:

1. **Conduct a hazard analysis** — identify biological, chemical, and physical hazards at every stage
2. **Determine Critical Control Points (CCPs)** — points where control is essential to prevent, eliminate, or reduce hazards to safe levels
3. **Establish critical limits** — measurable limits for each CCP (e.g., cooking temperature ≥ 75°C, frozen storage ≤ -18°C)
4. **Establish monitoring procedures** — how you will check CCPs (e.g., temperature logs, visual inspection, metal detection)
5. **Establish corrective actions** — what to do when a CCP exceeds critical limits
6. **Establish verification procedures** — confirm the HACCP system works (e.g., audits, testing, review of records)
7. **Establish record-keeping** — documentation proving the system is functioning

In Malaysia, HACCP certification is issued by **KKM (Ministry of Health)** under the HACCP Certification Scheme, as well as by private certification bodies. For frozen food manufacturers, KKM mandates HACCP certification — MeSTI alone is not sufficient for certain product categories.

## What Is ISO 22000?

**ISO 22000** is an international standard for **Food Safety Management Systems (FSMS)**. Published by the International Organization for Standardization, ISO 22000 incorporates HACCP principles but goes much further:

- **Full management system** — integrates with other ISO standards (ISO 9001 for quality, ISO 14001 for environment)
- **Interactive communication** — requires formal communication with suppliers, customers, and regulators
- **System management** — documented policies, management review meetings, internal audits, continual improvement
- **Pre-requisite programmes (PRPs)** — formalised GMP requirements
- **Traceability** — complete forward and backward traceability system
- **Emergency preparedness** — food defence, biovigilance, recall procedures
- **Risk-based thinking** — broader than HACCP's hazard analysis alone

ISO 22000 is recognised **globally**, making it essential for food businesses that export or supply to multinational companies.

## Key Differences: HACCP vs ISO 22000

| Aspect | HACCP | ISO 22000 |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Food safety hazards (CCPs) | Full Food Safety Management System |
| **Documentation** | HACCP plan + records | FSMS manual, policies, procedures, records |
| **Management involvement** | Not required | Required — management review, policy sign-off |
| **Internal audits** | Not mandatory | Mandatory — scheduled internal audits |
| **Communication** | Limited to HACCP team | Formal communication with all stakeholders |
| **Traceability** | Basic (one-up, one-down) | Comprehensive full-chain traceability |
| **International recognition** | High (codex-based) | Very high (ISO standard, GFSI-recognised) |
| **Certification body** | KKM or private | Private certification bodies only (SIRIM, TUV, Intertek, BSI) |
| **Cost** | RM5,000–15,000 | RM15,000–40,000 |
| **Implementation time** | 3–6 months | 6–18 months |
| **Maintenance** | Annual surveillance audit | Annual surveillance audit + 3-year recertification |

## Which Certification for Your Business?

### Frozen Food Manufacturers

**HACCP is mandatory.** KKM requires frozen food manufacturers (especially those producing ready-to-eat frozen foods) to hold HACCP certification. MeSTI may be sufficient for very small operations, but any business supplying to supermarkets, hotels, or other food businesses will need HACCP.

If you export frozen food (to Singapore, Middle East, EU, etc.), **ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000** is strongly recommended — many international buyers require it.

### Restaurant Chains & Central Kitchens

For a single restaurant outlet, **HACCP is optional** but becoming increasingly expected in the industry. However, for restaurant chains with a **central kitchen** supplying multiple outlets, KKM generally expects HACCP certification at the central kitchen level.

**ISO 22000** is recommended for:

- Large restaurant chains (10+ outlets)
- Restaurants in hotels and resorts
- Franchise operations with brand-wide quality standards
- Restaurants targeting corporate clients or government contracts

### Catering Businesses

Catering companies supplying to airlines, hospitals, schools, or government events often need **ISO 22000** — these clients specify it in their tender requirements. For general corporate and event catering, HACCP is usually sufficient.

### Small Restaurants and Cafés

A single-location small restaurant, café, or hawker stall does **not** typically need HACCP or ISO 22000. Your primary food safety obligation is:

- Valid food handling certificates for all staff
- MeSTI certification (if you have a production kitchen)
- Compliance with local council health inspections

Investing in HACCP without the volume or client demand to justify it may not be the best use of your money.

## Certification Bodies in Malaysia

### HACCP Certification

HACCP can be certified by **KKM directly** (government scheme, lower cost but longer timeline) or by private certification bodies:

- **SIRIM QAS International** — Malaysia's national certification body
- **TÜV SÜD Malaysia**
- **Intertek Malaysia**
- **BSI Group Malaysia**
- **Nexus Certification**

### ISO 22000 Certification

ISO 22000 is only issued by **accredited private certification bodies** (KKM does not issue ISO certificates):

- **SIRIM QAS International**
- **TÜV SÜD Malaysia**
- **TÜV Rheinland Malaysia**
- **Intertek Malaysia**
- **BSI Group Malaysia**
- **Lloyd's Register**

Always verify that your chosen certification body is **accredited by Standards Malaysia (Department of Standards Malaysia)** or an international accreditation body recognised through the IAF (International Accreditation Forum).

## How Long Does Certification Take?

| Phase | HACCP | ISO 22000 |
|---|---|---|
| Gap analysis & planning | 2–4 weeks | 4–8 weeks |
| System development & documentation | 4–8 weeks | 8–16 weeks |
| Implementation & staff training | 4–8 weeks | 8–16 weeks |
| Internal audit & management review | 1–2 weeks | 2–4 weeks |
| Certification audit (Stage 1 + Stage 2) | 1–2 weeks | 2–4 weeks |
| Corrective actions & close-out | 1–4 weeks | 1–4 weeks |
| **Total** | **3–6 months** | **6–18 months** |

For ISO 22000, the certification audit is conducted in two stages:

- **Stage 1**: Documentation review — auditor checks your FSMS documentation before visiting the site
- **Stage 2**: On-site audit — auditor verifies implementation at your premises

## Certification Validity and Maintenance

| | HACCP | ISO 22000 |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate validity | 3 years | 3 years |
| Surveillance audits | Annually | Annually |
| Recertification audit | Every 3 years (full audit) | Every 3 years (full audit) |

Both certifications require continuous maintenance. If you fail a surveillance audit, your certificate can be suspended or withdrawn.

## The GMP → MeSTI → HACCP → ISO 22000 Pathway

Many Malaysian food businesses follow this progression:

1. **MeSTI** — starting point, basic hygiene and safety (RM200–500, 3–6 months)
2. **GMP** — formalised good manufacturing practices (RM3,000–8,000, 3–6 months)
3. **HACCP** — hazard analysis and CCP monitoring (RM5,000–15,000, 3–6 months)
4. **ISO 22000** — full food safety management system (RM15,000–40,000, 6–18 months)

Each level builds on the one before it. There is no need to jump from MeSTI straight to ISO 22000 — the documentation, procedures, and staff training from HACCP make the ISO 22000 transition much smoother and cheaper.

## FAQ

### Do small restaurants need HACCP?

**Generally, no.** A single-location restaurant, café, or food stall with a simple kitchen does not need HACCP certification. Your regulatory obligations are:

- Food handling certificates for all staff
- MeSTI certification if you have a production kitchen
- Compliance with local council (PBT) health inspections
- Halal certification (if applicable)

HACCP becomes relevant when you start supplying food to other businesses, operating a central kitchen for multiple outlets, or when a client (corporate, hotel, supermarket) requires it as a condition of doing business.

### Can I skip HACCP and go straight to ISO 22000?

**Technically yes, but not recommended.** ISO 22000 incorporates HACCP principles, so you can theoretically implement ISO 22000 without prior HACCP certification. However:

- ISO 22000 is significantly more complex and costly
- The jump from no certification to ISO 22000 is difficult — there are many foundational elements (documentation culture, monitoring discipline, staff training) that are easier to build gradually through HACCP
- Most consultants recommend HACCP first, then ISO 22000 1–2 years later

If you already have strong food safety practices and documentation in place (e.g., experienced QA team, existing SOPs, regular audits), going directly to ISO 22000 is feasible but still challenging.

### How long is HACCP / ISO 22000 certification valid?

Both certifications are valid for **3 years**, subject to annual surveillance audits. If you pass all surveillance audits, you undergo a **full recertification audit** at the end of the 3-year cycle.

### Is HACCP recognised internationally?

**Yes.** HACCP is based on the Codex Alimentarius guidelines, which are the international food safety standards recognised by the World Trade Organization (WTO). Most countries accept HACCP certification. However, some international buyers (especially in the EU and Japan) increasingly request ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000, which are GFSI-benchmarked.

### What is FSSC 22000 and how does it differ from ISO 22000?

**FSSC 22000 (Food Safety System Certification)** is ISO 22000 plus additional requirements, specifically:

- ISO/TS 22002-1 (detailed PRP specification for food manufacturing)
- Additional FSSC-specific requirements (food defence, food fraud prevention, allergen management)

FSSC 22000 is fully recognised by the **Global Food Safety Initiative (GFSI)**, making it the preferred certification for exporting to markets where GFSI recognition is required. It is more demanding than ISO 22000 alone.

### How do I choose between SIRIM, TÜV, and Intertek?

All three are reputable and internationally recognised. Consider:

- **SIRIM QAS** — Malaysian government-linked, strong local reputation, competitive pricing, good for businesses primarily serving the Malaysian market
- **TÜV SÜD / TÜV Rheinland** — German certification bodies with strong international recognition, especially valued by European buyers
- **Intertek** — UK-based, strong in food sector globally, good for businesses exporting to multiple markets
- **BSI** — British Standards Institution, original authors of many ISO standards, premium brand with corresponding pricing

Request quotes from 2–3 bodies and compare not just price but also auditor availability, local office support, and turnaround time.

### Do I need a consultant to get HACCP or ISO 22000?

**For HACCP** — possible without a consultant if you have in-house food safety expertise. Many businesses appoint a trained HACCP team leader to develop and implement the system. KKM offers HACCP training programmes.

**For ISO 22000** — a consultant is strongly recommended. The documentation requirements, management system elements, and audit preparation are complex. A good consultant saves time and reduces the risk of failing the certification audit. Budget **RM8,000–15,000** for ISO 22000 consulting.

### What if I fail the certification audit?

If the auditor finds major non-conformances, you are given a timeframe to correct them (usually **30–90 days**). You must submit evidence of corrective actions. The certification body may conduct a follow-up audit (additional cost). If non-conformances are not resolved, the certification is not issued. Most first-time applicants face minor non-conformances — this is normal and expected.
