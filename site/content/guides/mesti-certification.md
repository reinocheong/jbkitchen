## What Is MeSTI Certification?

**MeSTI** stands for *Makanan Selamat Tanggungjawab Industri* (Safe Food — Industry Responsibility). It is a food safety certification scheme administered by the **Ministry of Health Malaysia (KKM)** through its Food Safety and Quality Division (BKKM).

MeSTI is designed for **small and medium-sized food businesses (SMEs)** that may not have the resources to pursue full HACCP certification. It ensures that food premises comply with basic hygiene and safety requirements under the **Food Hygiene Regulations 2009** and the **Food Act 1983**.

The certificate is valid for **3 years** (with renewal required before expiry) and is recognised nationwide. For frozen food manufacturers, central kitchens, and wholesale food handlers in Johor Bahru, MeSTI is often the first certification required before scaling to HACCP or ISO 22000.

## Who Needs MeSTI Certification?

MeSTI is mandatory or highly recommended for:

- **Food manufacturers** — including frozen food producers, sauces, snacks, baked goods, and ready-to-eat meals
- **Cold storage and frozen food warehouses** — any facility storing food products for distribution
- **Food wholesalers and distributors** — businesses that repackage or redistribute food
- **Central kitchens and commissaries** — supplying multiple restaurant outlets
- **Restaurant chains** — especially those with a central production kitchen
- **Home-based food businesses** — yes, even small-scale operations that sell commercially

If you manufacture, process, package, store, or distribute food in Malaysia, **KKM expects you to hold MeSTI certification**. Many local councils (PBT) in Johor also reference MeSTI as a requirement in business license renewals.

## MeSTI vs HACCP vs GMP — What's the Difference?

| Certification | Scope | Complexity | Typical Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **MeSTI** | Basic food safety & hygiene | Low-Medium | RM200–500 |
| **GMP** | Good Manufacturing Practice | Medium | RM3,000–8,000 |
| **HACCP** | Hazard Analysis Critical Control Point | High | RM5,000–15,000 |
| **ISO 22000** | Full Food Safety Management System | Very High | RM15,000–40,000 |

MeSTI is essentially **HACCP-lite** — it covers the fundamental principles without requiring the full documentation and hazard analysis framework. Think of it as the entry-level food safety certification. Many businesses start with MeSTI and later upgrade to HACCP or ISO 22000 as they grow.

## MeSTI Requirements Checklist

Before applying, ensure your premises meet these criteria:

### 1. Premises & Facilities
- Clean and well-maintained production/storage area
- Smooth, washable walls and floors (no exposed brick or porous surfaces)
- Adequate lighting (minimum 300 lux in processing areas)
- Proper ventilation to remove odours, steam, and heat
- Pest control system in place (documented)
- Separate hand-washing basin with soap and paper towels/hand dryer
- Toilet facilities that do NOT open directly into the processing area

### 2. Equipment & Utensils
- Food-grade materials only (stainless steel, food-grade plastic)
- Easy to clean and sanitise
- Regular maintenance schedule
- Dedicated storage for cleaning chemicals (separate from food)

### 3. Personnel Hygiene
- All food handlers must hold a valid **Food Handling Certificate** (Kursus Pengendali Makanan)
- Clean uniforms/aprons worn at all times
- Hairnets and covered shoes
- No jewellery, nail polish, or false nails
- Medical examination for food handlers (typhoid vaccination)

### 4. Raw Materials & Storage
- Approved suppliers with traceability records
- Incoming ingredient inspection procedure
- **Cold storage temperatures**: frozen at **-18°C or below**, chilled at **0–4°C**
- FIFO (First In, First Out) stock rotation
- All ingredients labelled with name, date received, and expiry

### 5. Documentation
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for all key processes
- Cleaning and sanitation schedules
- Pest control records
- Staff training records
- Traceability records (one step forward, one step back)

## MeSTI Application Process — Step by Step

### Step 1: Attend the MeSTI Training

Before applying, the business owner or a nominated representative must attend the **MeSTI training programme** conducted by KKM or an accredited training provider. This is a **2-day course** covering:

- Food safety principles
- Hygiene requirements
- Documentation and record-keeping
- Preparation for the audit

Upon completion, you receive a **certificate of attendance**, which is required for your application.

### Step 2: Prepare Your Documentation

Compile the following documents:

1. SSM business registration certificate (or Companies Commission registration)
2. Business license from local council (PBT)
3. Premises floor plan (showing workflow: receiving → storage → processing → packaging → finished goods)
4. List of all food products manufactured or handled
5. List of raw materials and suppliers
6. Food handler certificates for all staff
7. Typhoid vaccination records
8. Cleaning and sanitation schedule
9. Pest control contract or records

### Step 3: Submit Application to KKM

Applications are submitted to the **nearest District Health Office (Pejabat Kesihatan Daerah)**.

For Johor Bahru businesses, the office is:

**Pejabat Kesihatan Daerah Johor Bahru**
Jalan Abdul Samad, 80100 Johor Bahru, Johor
Tel: 07-224 5188

Submit your application form along with all supporting documents. The processing fee ranges from **RM200 to RM500** depending on the size and complexity of your operation.

### Step 4: Pre-Audit Preparation

Once your application is accepted, prepare for the audit:

- Conduct an internal walk-through using the MeSTI checklist
- Ensure all documentation is complete and accessible
- Brief all staff on what to expect during the audit
- Verify that cleaning and maintenance are up to date
- Check that all temperature logs are current

### Step 5: KKM On-Site Audit

A KKM food safety officer will visit your premises for an **on-site audit**, typically lasting **2–4 hours**. The auditor will:

- Walk through the entire facility (receiving, storage, processing, packaging, dispatch)
- Examine documentation (SOPs, records, logs)
- Interview staff about hygiene procedures
- Check equipment calibration and maintenance
- Verify pest control measures
- Test your traceability system (mock recall)

You will receive a score. If you meet the minimum threshold, the officer will recommend certification. Minor non-conformances must be corrected within the agreed timeframe (usually **14–30 days**).

### Step 6: Receive Your Certificate

If the audit is successful and any corrective actions are closed, KKM issues the **MeSTI certificate**. The certificate includes:

- Your company name and address
- Scope of certification (products covered)
- Certificate number
- Date of issue and expiry

## Cost Breakdown

| Item | Estimated Cost (RM) |
|---|---|
| MeSTI training (2-day course) | 200–500 |
| Application and audit fee (KKM) | 200–500 |
| Premises upgrades (if needed) | 1,000–10,000+ |
| Food handling certificates (per person) | 50–120 |
| Typhoid vaccination (per person) | 20–40 |

**Total typical cost: RM500–1,500** for a small operation with minimal upgrades needed.

## Renewal

MeSTI certificates are valid for **3 years**. Renewal follows the same process as the initial application — you must reapply, submit updated documents, and undergo a new audit. Start the renewal process at least **3 months before expiry** to avoid a gap in certification.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

Operating without MeSTI certification when required can result in:

- **Compound fines** up to RM10,000 under the Food Hygiene Regulations 2009
- Closure of premises under Section 11 of the Food Act 1983
- Products seized and destroyed
- Inability to renew local council business license
- Disqualification from government tenders and contracts
- Loss of halal certification (JAKIM requires food safety certification as a prerequisite)

## FAQ

### Do I need MeSTI if I already have HACCP?

**No.** If you already hold valid HACCP certification from an accredited body, KKM generally does not require separate MeSTI certification. HACCP is considered a higher standard that encompasses MeSTI requirements. However, some local councils may still ask for a copy of your MeSTI certificate — in this case, present your HACCP certificate and it should be accepted.

### How long does the MeSTI application process take?

From application to certification, expect **3 to 6 months**. The timeline depends on how quickly you can prepare your premises and documentation, and the availability of KKM auditors. The training (2 days), document preparation (2–4 weeks), and corrective actions after audit (2–4 weeks) are the main time drivers.

### Can a small home-based business apply for MeSTI?

**Yes.** MeSTI is specifically designed for small and medium businesses, including home-based operations. However, your home kitchen must meet the same hygiene and facility requirements as a commercial premises — separate processing area, washable surfaces, proper ventilation, no pets, and no domestic activities during production hours. Many home-based businesses in Johor begin with MeSTI as their first step toward formal certification.

### Is MeSTI mandatory for frozen food businesses?

**Yes.** If you manufacture, repackage, or distribute frozen food commercially in Malaysia, KKM requires you to have at least MeSTI certification. Frozen food is classified as a high-risk product, and food safety oversight is strict. Without MeSTI, your products cannot be sold through supermarkets, online platforms, or supplied to other businesses.

### Does MeSTI cover food delivery or catering?

MeSTI covers the **production facility** (kitchen, central kitchen, factory). If you operate a catering business with a central kitchen, that kitchen should be MeSTI-certified. For food delivery-only operations (no production), you may not need MeSTI, but your suppliers must be certified.

### Can I apply if my premises is rented?

**Yes.** As long as the rented premises meet MeSTI requirements and your tenancy agreement allows food production, you can apply. You must provide the tenancy agreement as part of your application documents.
